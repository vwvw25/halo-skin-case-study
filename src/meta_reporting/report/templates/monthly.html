Methodology

CM-LTV is 12-month contribution-margin LTV: net revenue less per-SKU COGS, a flat shipping cost and payment fees. Projection: a cohort observed to age t with realized CM c is projected as c × curve(12) / curve(t), where the curve is the mean cumulative CM per customer by tenure, pooled across the whole customer base. Cohorts already 12 months old are reported as realized. Target cohort: ≥3 orders in the first 90 days, average order value ≥ $62, and ≥1 premium-line purchase. CAC is cost per attributed new customer and runs ~18% above true CAC due to untagged acquisitions.
